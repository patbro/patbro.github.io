I'm Patrick, I live in the beautiful city of Heeze and I dedicate most of my time to working at [Wolky Tolky](https://wolkytolky.com) as a mechatronics engineer by day. A place where I focus on turning technology into real-world value, and sometimes loose track of time because I do what I like most.

The other hours of the day you can find me building oil separators and pickling bath solutions at [vanbroeckhuijsenvof.nl](https://vanbroeckhuijsenvof.nl), or exploring entrepeneurship at [Antrum Technologies](https://antrum-technologies.nl) with for example [Eindeloos Licht](https://eindelooslicht.nl).

Over the weekend, I usually spend my time on a combination of software and electronic projects. These projects range from reverse-engineering the tennis court reservation app, to developing electronics for my home automation. The latter I might write an article about some time. Most of the software I write is open sourced on my [GitHub](https://github.com/patbro).

When I'm not building new things, I like to spend time with my girlfriend and dog. Usually, when you can't find me behind my desk, I'm breaking a sweat by going for a run before dawn or playing tennis.
